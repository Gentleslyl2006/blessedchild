About the school

Blessed Child Model Group of Schools is a government-approved school in Nkpikpa, Ogbaru, Anambra State, offering quality education from Pre-Nursery through Secondary School. Guided by our motto, "My Child is My Pride," we are committed to raising godly, disciplined children who excel academically. With two campuses, a caring team of teachers, and a safe, nurturing environment, we help every child grow in character, knowledge, and confidence. We also provide WAEC preparation for our senior students and a school bus service for added convenience. Give your child the best education for a brighter tomorrow.
